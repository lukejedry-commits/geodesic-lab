Space view and time view

For slow motion, gravity comes almost entirely from slowed time, not curved space. Slow orbits are mostly slowed time: the Newtonian limit is gtt ≈ −(1+2Φ), and spatial curvature enters only at order v². A marble rolling on the surface of the left picture under a downward pull does not reproduce orbits. (In the weak field, light bending 4M/b is half from time, 2M/b, and half from space, 2M/b.)

2+1 gravity: no pull, only lensing

A point mass m in 2+1 gravity leaves space flat everywhere except at m. Cut a wedge of deficit angle δ = 8πGm and glue its edges: a cone. Geodesics are straight lines on the unrolled plane. Particles at rest stay at rest (green dots do not move), there is no horizon, no static-clock slowdown (clock rate 1 everywhere), no orbits. Two parallel rays passing either side of m cross: lensing without a pull. Range shown: δ < π so the rays meet the wedge ahead.
